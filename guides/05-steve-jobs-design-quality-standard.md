## 📌 The 4 Pillars of Product Design & Quality

1. **Simplicity is Supreme**: Strip away friction, noise, and unnecessary steps; make workflows intuitive and effortless.
2. **Uncompromising Aesthetic Excellence**: Deliver visual designs that WOW at first glance (glassmorphism, curated dark palettes `#0a0d14`, geometric typography, smooth HSL neon glows, and micro-animations).
3. **End-to-End Perfection ("Paint the Back of the Fence")**: Code architecture, type definitions, data schemas, and UI components are treated with the exact same precision and craft.
4. **Ruthless Curation & Focus**: Say no to 1,000 feature compromises; deliver focused, high-signal, "insanely great" solutions.
